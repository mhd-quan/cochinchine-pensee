# v0.7.2 — Essay quotations and author archives

## Editorial discovery

The homepage now places a restrained print-style discovery spread between Recent
Essays and From the Stacks. Its left column shows one complete quotation with the
matching cover and essay title; the whole feature opens the essay reader.
The right column lists the publication's two current authors, their published essay
counts and latest work. The spread becomes one readable column on smaller screens.

The quotation corpus contains two manually selected, source-verified excerpts for
each of 57 eligible published essays (114 excerpts total). The earliest Minh Tuệ
essay remains published and visible in its author archive but is deliberately
excluded from the homepage quotation pool. A deterministic Vietnam
calendar chooses one shared quotation for every visitor each day. Articles advance
one per day; a later pass through the archive advances each article to its second
excerpt. Reloads, tabs, Astro navigation and browser history retain that day's item.
An open page checks again after focus, visibility and page-show events so crossing
midnight updates the spread without an automatic reading-time cycle. Only the
selected responsive image is added to the active document. With JavaScript disabled,
the statically generated fallback remains on the build date until the next deployment.

## Author archives

M. Q. Doan and Adler now have permanent filtered archives at `/authors/m-q-doan`
and `/authors/adler`. The existing `/authors` overview remains and links to both.
Each archive is generated from published content, so direct visits, reloads and Back
navigation work without client-side filtering or fabricated profiles.

## Editorial type

Article titles now use a self-hosted Playfair Display SC face with real regular and
bold WOFF2 subsets. Homepage and archive titles keep their source text and use the
font's native small-cap forms. Reader typeface choices are Garamond, New York and
San Serif, backed by Bricolage Grotesque. Existing Source Serif preferences migrate
to Garamond, while Bricolage loads only when selected.

## Validation

- Every quotation is checked against normalized rendered paragraph text from its
  source essay, and draft or announced work cannot enter the pool.
- Lifecycle checks cover the Vietnam timezone boundary, independent visitors,
  direct access, reload, Astro navigation, Back and BFCache, including
  article/title/author/image lockstep and an open page crossing midnight.
- Static archive checks verify each author URL, canonical and published essay set.
- Production type-check reports zero errors and warnings; the build produces
  82 pages and all 33 automated tests pass. The changed files
  pass focused Biome checks; the repository-wide lint command still reports
  pre-existing formatting diagnostics in unrelated files.
- Browser review covers 320, 390, 768 and 1280px widths, both authors, the longest
  Vietnamese and English excerpts, no-script fallback and image request behavior.
  Reader checks confirm saved preferences, Sage, no horizontal overflow and
  Bricolage loading only on selection. External analytics collection is mocked
  during browser QA because its endpoint cannot resolve in the test environment.
