# v0.7.4 — Homepage quotation composition

From the Essays now matches From the Stacks in heading size, weight, tracking,
and placement of its bottom rule. Redundant quote borders and the separator
above the book section are removed, and the Authors heading aligns with Essays.

The quotation artwork fills the right side behind the text on desktop, with
edge fades strongest toward the lower left. The essay title sits at the bottom
right. Mobile uses rounded corners and a stronger fade behind the quotation
for legibility. Responsive image sizes reflect the larger desktop artwork.

Validation: production build, 38 existing tests, Astro check (zero errors or
warnings), and visual checks at desktop and mobile sizes.
